# Creative Design Thinking for Data-Dense Web Apps — CRITICAL

Design the application for **high information density, fast scanning, and efficient financial workflows**.

* **Compact Layouts:** Prefer compact, information-rich layouts over oversized cards and excessive whitespace.
* **Clear Hierarchy:** Make important financial information immediately distinguishable from supporting details.
* **Key Information:** Prioritize amount, date, category, account, totals, and status.
* **Numeric Alignment:** Use `tabular-nums` and right-align monetary and numerical values for easy comparison.
* **Useful Visual Cues:** Use status indicators, trends, progress, and category accents only when they communicate meaningful information.
* **Real-World Data:** Design for long names, large values, negative balances, many records, and empty datasets.
* **Complete States:** Handle loading, empty, error, validation, no-results, disabled, and extreme-data states.
* **Mobile Efficiency:** Prioritize essential information, thumb-friendly actions, and progressive disclosure on mobile.
* **Desktop Density:** Use additional desktop space for more useful information rather than simply increasing spacing or component size.
* **Fast Workflows:** Keep Add, Search, Filter, Category, Account, and Date actions quick and accessible.
* **Purposeful Interaction:** Use subtle feedback and contextual interactions without unnecessary animation.
* **Minimal Decoration:** Avoid decorative UI, excessive whitespace, unnecessary navigation, and anything that reduces useful data density.

**Core Principle:** Optimize every screen for **clarity, comparison, scan speed, and action**.
